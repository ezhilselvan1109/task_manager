## 3. Key Governance Rules & Guarantees Validated
1. **Binding Antigravity Rules**: 10 inviolable engineering rules (`RULE-01` through `RULE-10`) codified, including documentation-first discipline, zero stubs, and memory caps under 5.25GB.
2. **Quality Gates Enforced**: 5-stage quality gates spanning design, local pre-commit, CI build, Docker packaging, and runtime health probes.
3. **Definition of Ready & Done**: Standardized checklists ensure stories are fully defined prior to sprint intake and strictly verified prior to pull request merge.
4. **GitFlow & Conventional Commits**: Clean branching taxonomy and structured commit headers ensure traceability and automated release notes generation.
5. **Testing Architecture**: Test pyramid baselined with JUnit 5 unit tests, slice tests, and Testcontainers integration tests targeting $\ge 80\%$ line coverage.
